## Research notes (October 2026)

- No public web presence found under "Gogo Thudi". No Google results, no Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or directory listings.
- sa-psychics.com blocks automated access (Cloudflare), and the Wayback Machine was unreachable, so her old profile and reviews could not be pulled. Profiles on that site live at `sa-psychics.com/GetBusiness/{NAME}`.
- Searching "traditional healer Pretoria" or "sangoma Pretoria North" returns mostly spam listings: phone numbers in titles, "bring back lost lover", guaranteed money and court-case wins. That is the competition.
- That is the opening. A calm, credible site with real reviews and clear booking looks different from everything else on page one. The copy deliberately avoids guaranteed outcomes and medical cure claims.
