Most cold messages fail for one reason

They sound like a pitch. People can tell within one line, and they stop reading

The 7 below don't, because they're written the way you'd say it across a table. A few things they all have in common, so you can write your own later:

• Short. Two or three lines. If it needs scrolling on a phone, it's too long
• The small words stay in. "Normally", "I think", "kind of". Tight writing reads as written. Loose writing reads as a person
• One ask at a time. Never two questions in one message
• Easy to answer. Something they can reply to in five seconds

Swap "Thabo" and the details for your own. Keep the shape

1

The first message

Hi Thabo, saw you've just taken on a second site in Pretoria, congrats. Are you finding new work for it mostly through referrals at the moment?

Why it works: One real detail about them, one easy question. No pitch. They can answer it in five seconds

2

The bump

Hey Thabo, just bumping this in case it got buried. No stress if now's not a good time

Why it works: Send it 3 working days later. It's short and it gives them a way out, which is exactly why people reply to it

3

When they ask "how much?"

Fair question. It normally sits between R11,000 and R22,000 depending on what you need, and I'd rather tell you that now than waste your time. What's the main thing you'd want sorted first?

Why it works: Say the number early. Dodging it reads as hiding something

4

When they say "send me some info"

Sure, happy to. So I don't send you a generic PDF, what's the one thing you'd want it to answer?

Why it works: "Send info" is usually a polite no. This turns it back into a conversation

5

When they say "not right now"

Totally get it. Would it be ok if I checked back in around March? No pressure either way

Why it works: You get a yes to a later date, and you can put it in the diary

6

Asking for the call

Sounds like it might be worth a proper chat. Would a quick 30 minutes this Thursday or Friday work for you?

Why it works: Two days, not a calendar link and not "whenever suits". Easy to say yes to

7

The honest exit

Hey Thabo, I'll leave it here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If finding new clients becomes a thing later on, I'm around, let me know if I'm wrong about the timing

Why it works: After 3 touches and no reply. People reply to this one more than any other. Letting go makes it easy for them to come back

The 3 rules that matter more than the words

1Send about 10 a week, every week. Consistency beats bursts. 10 a week for 3 months beats 200 in one afternoon
2Write down who replied and what they said. Even a spreadsheet works. If it isn't written down, the follow-up doesn't happen
3Follow up 3 times. Most replies come on the 2nd or 3rd message, not the first
